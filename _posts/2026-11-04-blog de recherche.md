*Femmes en voix – Voix de femmes* is a research blog hosted on Hypotheses. It focuses on the project **women and feminisms in the public sphere, exploring discourses, narratives and representations from the nineteenth and twentieth centuries**.

## What you will find

The blog shares project news, session announcements and reports.

It follows the seminar *Voix de femmes. Regards interdisciplinaires sur le genre* at Université Côte d'Azur.

It also includes a **thematic map of the lectures** - in Italy, for now - which offers a way to explore the main topics they cover.

## Explore the map

You can also explore the [project map](https://femmesenvoix.hypotheses.org/carte-thematique-des-conferences) directly on this site.
