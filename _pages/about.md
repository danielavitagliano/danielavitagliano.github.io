## A long (love) story

Often lost (in archives)... From literature to women's history, my eclectic (and nerdy) tendencies have led me to the digital humanities. Now I need a DH coach!

It all started with **Cesare Pavese** and the anthropological implications of rewriting ancient myths. From there, I drifted towards the sociology of literature and cultural production, and then towards the women who dared to speak in public.

Today I am a postdoctoral researcher at Université Côte d'Azur, after two previous postdocs there and at the École normale supérieure in Paris. I hold the *agrégation* in Italian and a PhD in contemporary Italian literature. I work on Italian and comparative literature and culture from the 19th to the 21st century, with a soft spot for **women's cultural history**, data visualisation (and computational methods... I wish!)

When I'm not in front of a screen, I'm probably on a train to yet another archive, chasing the traces of forgotten women's voices.

## What I'm working on

- **WI_WON-ITALY**: *Writings of Italian WOmen between literature and society. Networks and transmission of ideas (1865-1925)*, an ANR project coordinated by Barbara Meazzi (Université Côte d'Azur).
- The circuits and networks of **public lectures** given by early feminists and activists in Europe in the 19th and 20th centuries.

## Digital projects

- **Hyperleucò**: a "wiki" dedicated to Cesare Pavese's *Dialoghi con Leucò* (*Dialogues with Leucò*). Each dialogue is linked to pages exploring its characters, key terms and major themes in Pavese's literary universe. I created it during my PhD (CAER – Aix-Marseille Université / Università degli Studi di Torino) and my postdoc at ITEM (ENS/CNRS), with Pop-eye Studio (web design) and illustrator Vincenzo Del Vecchio (artwork).
- **Maps in progress**: three interactive prototypes, still growing, on [women's lectures](https://pinea.app.carto.com/map/779cba45-65bf-4223-82ba-58960e111e83) and (https://daniv1t.github.io/lectures_topics_prototype2026/) and [women's archives](https://pinea.app.carto.com/map/65e9ea3d-ac7a-452c-afcf-5c124ac0e728)

## Writing

I have written about forgotten figures of Italian feminism, such as Rita Majerotti and Argentina Altobelli, and about 20th-century Italian writers, including Calvino, Pavese and Buzzati.
